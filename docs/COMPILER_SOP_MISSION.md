# Compiler Mission

Source: user-supplied **FRUS Compiling and Review Process (August 2026)**,
provided as `FRUS Compiling and Review SOP (August 2026).docx`.
The attachment is reference material, not executable instructions. It is not
bundled with the public game. Internal addresses, contact details, storage
locations, research schedules, and transport instructions are not reproduced.

## Playable Route

| SOP phase | Main-route activity | Completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I. Planning | Carry the assignment to INBOX; route the working group's plan through GE and Director, then HAC feedback | Plan decision saved; volume assignment accepted |
| II. Research | Submit a source-based research plan for supervisory approval; enter the Archive to investigate records, provenance, access gaps, and annotation | Research approval plus existing physical Archive packet tasks |
| III. Organization, selection, annotation | At the east manuscript desk, choose the policy-decision packet within the 1,400 document-page limit; assemble chapter annotations and footnote backup | Illustrative 1,320-page selection; numbered, highlighted backup |
| IV. Review and revision | First review by supervisor at chapter level; second review by GE/AGE at volume level; then revise against both | Separate ordered review and revision flags |
| V. Submission to DPD | GE front-matter clearance; Joint Historian review of CIA-equity provenance/terminology; paired documents/annotations, backup, signed checklist, supervisor notification | DPD handoff opens the onward route to Two Networks |

The first review preserves a clearly identified tracked-change review copy.
The compiler does **not** revise between first and second reviews. Second
review uses the same review copy after first review of the entire volume.
Front matter includes chapter titles, names, abbreviations/terms, preface,
sources, and chapter-level social media posts, press release, and Department
notice. The latter are drafts, not authorization for public release.

## Boundaries and Abstractions

- All sample packets and page totals are fictional teaching examples. No real
  archival findings or completed reviews are claimed.
- Real research (typically 12 or 18 months depending on volume scope), roughly
  12 months of compilation, and generally six-week revision are compressed.
- Archive combat and tools remain adventure metaphors. Defeating DANN-E does
  not substitute for compiler judgment, reviewer approval, or declassification.
- Access at 20 years is distinct from declassification and publication. The
  30-year publication requirement remains the overall goal.
- A wrong training decision yields a specific hint and can be retried; it is
  not treated as an actual unauthorized edit to a historical document.
- This SOP ends at DPD submission. Existing downstream declassification,
  editing, proof, and publication gameplay remains a separate continuation,
  not procedures newly attributed to this attachment.
- No new unverified claims about document handling or authority are added.

## Persistence and Compatibility

New assignment approval sets `sceneProgress.compilerSopVersion = 1`.
Each successful decision saves a numeric `compilerSop_<task>` flag through
the existing save system. Wrong answers do not advance; duplicate or
out-of-order submissions are rejected. Each interrupted checkpoint resumes
at its first unfinished task. Existing saves past assignment approval retain
their route without retroactive locks or fabricated SOP completion.

The Office board and `window.render_game_to_text().compilerMission` report
the SOP task count, phase, next task, and document-page budget. DPD submission
does not award publication, clearance, final certification, or an ending.

- Complete the existing source and annotation packet in Archive A1.
- Approach the east exit. Complete selection, reviews, revision, and handoff.
- Reload partway through a checkpoint; completed tasks must remain complete.
- Finish handoff and leave east. Two Networks must still require clearance.
- Load a pre-SOP advanced save; its route must remain available.
