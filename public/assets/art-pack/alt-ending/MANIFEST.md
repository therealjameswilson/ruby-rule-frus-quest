# Alt Ending Art Pack

Original repository-local 16-bit pixel art for the contested declassification ending.

## Assets

- `bg_interagency_review_room.png` — Buckram Gate alternate background for contested publication review.
- `stamp_under_appeal.png` — visible process stamp for the under-appeal publication branch.
- `volume_contested_redacted.png` — contested FRUS volume prize without the Great Seal.

## Usage

These assets are loaded through `ALT_ENDING_ASSETS` in `src/assets/registry.ts`.
