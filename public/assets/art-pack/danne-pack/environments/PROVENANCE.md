# Black Vault materials — 2026-09-24

Original slate material generated with OpenAI image generation for Ruby Rule, then resampled to 768x768 without changing its artwork. Source: exec-ef1406e2-3ecb-476a-972a-82436b4b0fe5.png. Prompt requested top-down seamless blue charcoal slate, fine grain and subtle brass flecks, no objects, shadows, lettering or walls.

The arena is composed at runtime from this material and original Canvas geometry in vaultEnvironment.ts. Raised obstacles use the authoritative collider footprints. The static image is cached at 768x720 and rendered as one object.
