# Volume Assembly Mini-Pack

Original repository-local 16-bit pixel-art placeholders for the FRUS binding ceremony.

- `volume_assembly_hud_bar.png` — 90x14 HUD progress strip with five slots.
- `volume_binding_animation_6x128.png` — 6 frames, 128x128 each, left-to-right binding sequence.
- `completed_volume_hero.png` — 128x128 final ruby buckram FRUS volume sprite.
