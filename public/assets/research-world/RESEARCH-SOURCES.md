# Research World: locations and holdings

Checked against official sources on September 22, 2026. The seven game areas are a compressed, freely accessible travel map, not a navigable street map. Buildings are original illustrative miniatures rather than architectural reconstructions. The research exercises and DANN-E dialogue are fiction. A discovery is a research lead, not an acquired or cleared document.

Ford's research library is represented in Ann Arbor, distinct from the museum in Grand Rapids. The region groupings compress journeys across states; they do not suggest these libraries are within walking distance of Washington. Repository staff, catalogs and finding aids establish access to particular collections.

## Artwork

Three original PNGs contain a landscape and 32 atlas cells: 16 landmarks, four civilian DANN-E directions, four trees, a bench, lamp, birdbath, rail station, satchel, finding-aid book, signpost and archivist. All are used in the scene. [Full generation prompts](ART-PROMPTS.md) record the built-in image-generation requests. PNG originals are preserved; the game reads atlas frames without rewriting the images.

## Playing

Use the green OUTSIDE door on the west side of the Office, even before completing the tutorial. Walk between Potomac Green, Capital Commons and Maryland Grove. Use A near buildings, DANN-E, the journal or rail station. Rail travel is free. The rail menu has a touch X and Escape to cancel. Each distant region has a DC sign; DC areas have an OFFICE sign. Discoveries save through the existing save system and do not grant production stamps or bypass dungeon approvals.
