Collections behind the diplomatic record

Research records dated 1989–2008. Check your email for your volume assignment. Repository visits and collection leads do not establish a retrieved or cleared document.

Use only in-period records from collections that span a longer period. Keep original collection titles, FOIA identifiers and release dates intact. A later release date is not the historical record date.

Library of Congress and Archives I remain research inquiry stops; no verified in-period folder packet is supplied here. Ask staff for dated inventories and access arrangements. Department of State RG 59 records belong at Archives II.

Open NARA Scout · 1989–2001 to scout declassified records, withdrawal sheets and unprocessed series. Scout currently focuses on Bush 41 and Clinton collections; check the holding repository and individual document dates. Its supported period is narrower than this game’s 1989–2008 window.
